Problem

Small shops in Jakarta sent invoices by WhatsApp and chased payment by hand. Average time to get paid was 13 days, and owners lost track of who owed what.

The goal was a tool a shop owner could learn in ten minutes, that sends a payable link, and reminds politely without the owner doing anything.
